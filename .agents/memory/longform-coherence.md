---
name: Long-form coherence
description: Why long screenplay generation must be globally constrained and audited
---
Treat production chunks as boundaries within one work, never as standalone documents with repeated introductions and conclusions. Adapt the supplied cross-chunk method to screenplay character identities, chronology, established facts, unresolved setups and payoffs.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected long outputs assembled from repetitive independent mini-documents and supplied a three-pass coherence method as the intended approach.

**How to apply:** Changes to generation must retain a global plan, actual-text continuity deltas, saved intermediate work, and a global audit with targeted repairs before declaring the result complete. A large output limit alone does not satisfy this requirement.

Generative audits also need grounding: distinguish actual contradictions and repeated scenes from harmless changes in scene allocation, off-page events, and intentional callbacks.

**Why:** In real adaptation runs, reviewers sometimes treated their own soft outline as immutable evidence and requested conflicting scene relocations. Passing an audit cannot be manufactured by simply disabling checks, but an unsupported criticism is not reliable evidence either.

**How to apply:** Future audit/repair work should require specific textual evidence for substantive issues and evaluate the actual screenplay against user requirements and established facts, rather than enforcing every detail of an initial production outline.

Saving unfinished writing is separate from approving a final screenplay. Users must be able to suspend and retain the text already produced without passing the final coherence audit.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested suspension with saved output, separately from stopping and starting again.

**How to apply:** Keep partial writing accessible for copying/downloading and clearly labeled unfinished. Preserve the saved snapshot during resumed work; do not weaken the final approval checks to make saving possible.
